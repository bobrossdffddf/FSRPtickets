RDM (Random Death Match) means killing someone without a valid roleplay reason. It ruins fair gameplay and realistic scenes. Examples: shooting a random driver at a gas station, or killing someone just for walking past you.
VDM (Vehicle Death Match) means using a vehicle to kill or hurt players without roleplay reason. Examples: running over people on purpose at spawn, or using a car to ram pedestrians for fun.
FRP (Fail Roleplay) means acting unrealistically or ignoring roleplay logic. Players violate realism by doing impossible actions or ignoring danger. Examples: jumping off a building and surviving normally
LTA (Leave to Avoid) means leaving the game to escape consequences in roleplay. Eg disconnecting during an arrest, or leaving during a traffic stop to avoid a ticket.
Metagaming means using information your character should not know from outside sources. This gives unfair advantages in RP. Examples: using Discord call info to find criminals, or reading stream chat to locate another player.
Powergaming means forcing unrealistic actions or controlling another player unfairly. Eg escaping handcuffs unrealistically.
NLR (New Life Rule) means after respawning, your character forgets the previous life and situation. Examples: not going back to a shootout, and not attacking the person who killed you earlier.
FearRP means valuing your character’s life and acting scared in dangerous situations. Eg when held at gunpoint, or surrendering when heavily outnumbered.
Cop baiting means intentionally irritating police just to start chases or reactions without good RP. This wastes time.Examples: driving circles around officers while honking, or speeding past police to get chased.
Combat Logging means leaving the game during an active roleplay or combat situation. Eg include disconnecting during a gunfight or leaving while being arrested
